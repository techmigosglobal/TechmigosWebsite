End-to-end Requirements & Process

Use this as your mini PRD before turning this HTML into a full web app.

Current version: This is a browser-only prototype. Data stays on the same device/browser. For team access, login, cloud backup, GST, automatic invoice numbering, or payment status sync, connect it to a backend such as Supabase/Firebase later.
Invoice Module Flow
  1. Enter company details, client details, invoice number and invoice date.
  2. Upload logo, payment QR/scanner image and authorised signature.
  3. Add service rows with title, description, quantity, unit, rate and amount.
  4. System calculates total, received amount and balance amount automatically.
  5. Preview the invoice in a TechMigos-style bordered layout.
  6. Click Generate Invoice PDF, then choose Save as PDF.
Financial Tracker Flow
  1. Record every income or expense with date, amount, category, mode, party and reference.
  2. Attach proof image/PDF such as payment screenshot, receipt, bill, UPI confirmation, etc.
  3. View records in a spreadsheet-like table with proof thumbnails or proof links.
  4. Filter by search, month and transaction type.
  5. Export CSV/Excel for accountant review, or backup/restore JSON with proof attachments.
Recommended Future Backend Version
  1. Authentication: admin login, accountant login, read-only client view.
  2. Database: invoices, invoice_items, clients, transactions, proof_files, company_settings.
  3. Storage: store logos, signatures, QR codes and transaction proofs safely.
  4. Automation: auto invoice number, monthly recurring invoice, due reminders, payment received status.
  5. Reports: monthly P&L, client dues, category-wise expenses, proof audit export.
