A redesign should improve the experience without making search engines or returning users lose their way. Treat content, structure, performance and redirects as one project.

## Inventory before changing

List important URLs, search traffic, backlinks, forms, integrations and pages people use to contact you. Keep useful content even when the visual system changes. Map every retired URL to the closest truthful destination.

## Rebuild the information hierarchy

Use one clear H1, descriptive headings, meaningful links and page titles that match search intent. Write for the person who arrives from a query, not only the person who already knows your brand.

## Protect accessibility

Check keyboard order, focus states, contrast, labels, alt text, zoom and reduced motion. Test real tasks rather than only running a scanner. See the [accessible responsive planning guide](/blog/accessible-responsive-websites).

## Protect performance

Reserve media space, prioritise the first visible image, limit third-party scripts and lazy-load below-the-fold content. Measure the production build on a mid-range mobile device.

## Launch with a watch list

Monitor 404s, index coverage, form submissions, conversions and performance after release. Keep a rollback path and a short log of changes. [Explore our web development service](/services#web).
