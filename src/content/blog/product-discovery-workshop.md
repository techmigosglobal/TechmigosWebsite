A product discovery workshop is a structured conversation that makes assumptions visible before they become expensive code. It is not a slide presentation and it is not a promise that every requested feature will ship.

## Bring the right people

Include the person who owns the business outcome, someone close to users, a delivery decision-maker and a technical voice. The group should be small enough to decide and broad enough to expose constraints.

## Work from evidence

Review current workflows, support questions, analytics and existing systems. Write down what is known, what is assumed and what must be tested. This prevents the loudest opinion from becoming the product strategy.

## Map the first journey

Choose one user and one valuable outcome. Map their trigger, actions, decisions, failure points and result. Then mark where a simple prototype or technical spike can reduce uncertainty.
