Firebase and Supabase can both help a small team ship quickly. The better choice depends on the product’s data shape, security model, realtime behaviour and the operational skills available after launch.

## Compare the data model

Firebase’s document-oriented services can fit event-heavy and realtime experiences. Supabase’s PostgreSQL foundation is attractive when relational queries, SQL workflows and portability matter. Start with the queries your product must answer, not a technology popularity list.

## Compare identity and access

Write down roles, ownership, team boundaries and administrative actions. Then check how each platform expresses and tests those rules. A familiar auth screen is not enough if the data boundary is unclear.

## Include operations

Consider backups, local development, migrations, logs, quotas, billing and export. The cheapest first month is not the same as the lowest long-term risk. Prototype one sensitive workflow before committing.

## Avoid migration theatre

Do not choose a platform because a future rewrite sounds impressive. Choose explicit boundaries, keep data contracts documented and build a small exit path where it matters. Our [backend architecture guide](/blog/backend-architecture-growing-products) covers the surrounding decisions.
