Performance is a product quality attribute and a search consideration. A fast page helps people understand the offer before they lose patience, especially on mobile networks.

## Know the three signals

LCP measures how quickly the main content appears, INP reflects interaction responsiveness and CLS measures unexpected movement. Use the practical targets of under 2.5 seconds LCP, under 200 milliseconds INP and under 0.1 CLS as release goals.

## Budget the expensive work

Set limits for JavaScript, fonts, images and third-party scripts. Prioritise the first visible product screen, reserve media dimensions and lazy-load content that is below the fold. A smaller bundle often improves both speed and clarity.

## Measure real conditions

Test a production build on a mid-range mobile device and a constrained network. Lab scores explain a bottleneck; field data tells you whether users feel it. Compare the same page before and after a change.

## Protect interaction quality

Avoid long main-thread tasks, expensive scroll handlers and animations that force layout. Use transforms and opacity for motion. The [accessible responsive website approach](/blog/accessible-responsive-websites) and performance work should reinforce each other.
