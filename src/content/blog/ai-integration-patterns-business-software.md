Useful AI features fit inside a real workflow. They reduce a costly step, improve a decision or help someone find the right information; they do not exist merely because a text box can be added.

## Start with bounded patterns

Good first patterns include extracting fields from documents, classifying incoming requests, drafting a response for review, summarising a known record and searching a trusted knowledge base. Each pattern has a clear input, output and owner.

## Keep humans in the loop

Show sources, confidence or uncertainty where it matters. Let people edit, reject and report a result. Log the input, model version and final decision for workflows that affect customers, money or access.

## Protect data

Classify what can leave your systems, redact sensitive fields and define retention. Keep prompt construction and permissions on the server side. AI does not remove the need for normal application security.

## Measure usefulness

Track completion time, correction rate, acceptance rate and harmful misses. A demo that sounds clever is not evidence that the workflow improved. Our [AI integration service](/services#ai) starts with the workflow and the guardrails.
