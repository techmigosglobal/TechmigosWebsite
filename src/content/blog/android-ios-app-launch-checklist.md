An app launch is an operating event, not only a store submission. Your team needs to know what users will see, how issues will be reported and how a release can be corrected safely.

## Product readiness

Complete the core workflow on supported devices and OS versions. Test first run, sign-in, permissions, offline recovery, deep links, notifications and destructive actions. Remove unfinished screens and placeholder copy.

## Store readiness

Prepare accurate names, descriptions, screenshots, icons, privacy disclosures, support URLs and review notes. Make the store promise match the experience. Regional availability and payment rules should be checked early.

## Operational readiness

Set up analytics, crash reporting, feature flags, support routing and an incident owner. Define what you will watch during the first day and how you will pause or roll back a risky release.

## Communication readiness

Give users a concise explanation of what changed. Tell support what is new, what is known and how to collect useful evidence. [Our QA service](/services#qa) can help turn this into a release record.
