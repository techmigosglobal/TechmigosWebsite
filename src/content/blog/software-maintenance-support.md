Launch is the start of a product’s operating life. Maintenance protects the system, while support protects the people who depend on it and supplies evidence for the next product decision.

## Keep the foundations healthy

Schedule dependency updates, security review, backups, restore tests, certificate renewal and access audits. Record who owns each task and what “healthy” looks like.

## Monitor the experience

Track errors, latency, failed jobs, storage, authentication and important business workflows. Alerts should identify an owner and a useful next action rather than create noise.

## Make support learnable

Capture the user’s goal, expected result, actual result, timestamp and relevant identifiers. Group repeated questions into product improvements instead of answering the same confusion forever.
