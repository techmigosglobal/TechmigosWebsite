## Role Overview

Help businesses discover TechMigos and understand where our software services can help. You’ll connect marketing work to sales follow-up, from writing a campaign and measuring its response to qualifying an inquiry and booking a discovery conversation.

## Responsibilities

- Turn service offerings and approved project examples into clear landing-page copy, emails, and social content.
- Plan and run campaigns for web, mobile, desktop, and software integration services.
- Respond to inbound inquiries, qualify leads, and hand useful context to the business development team.
- Track inquiries, booked calls, and qualified opportunities so the team can see which campaigns drive meaningful conversations.
- Test messaging and calls to action, and share what you learn with the team.

## Qualifications

- Experience in marketing, lead generation, sales, or a combination of those areas.
- Ability to write clear business copy and explain a software service in plain language.
- Comfort using analytics, a CRM, or spreadsheets to track results and follow-ups.
- Ability to manage several campaigns or inquiries while keeping records up to date.

## Helpful Experience

- Marketing for B2B services, software, or a digital agency.
- Hands-on experience with SEO content, email campaigns, LinkedIn, or landing-page testing.
