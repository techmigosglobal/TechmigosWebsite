## Role Overview

Help TechMigos find businesses that need a website, mobile app, desktop tool, or software integration. You’ll lead early conversations, identify the outcome the client needs, and work with the delivery team to shape a realistic proposal.

## Responsibilities

- Research prospective clients and partners, and write outreach tailored to their business.
- Run discovery calls to understand the client’s workflow, budget, timeline, and decision process.
- Bring clear discovery notes to design and engineering so they can assess scope, dependencies, and delivery risks.
- Coordinate proposals, manage follow-ups, and hand over agreed expectations when a project starts.
- Keep the sales pipeline current and share what you learn from won, lost, and stalled opportunities.

## Qualifications

- Experience in business development, client acquisition, or account management for services.
- Clear written and verbal communication and the ability to ask practical discovery questions.
- Familiarity with software projects and the ability to explain scope and trade-offs in plain language.
- Organized follow-up habits and comfort with a CRM or another structured pipeline tool.

## Helpful Experience

- Selling web development, app development, consulting, or other technical services.
- Preparing proposals or developing referral and agency partnerships.
