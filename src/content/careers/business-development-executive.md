## Role Overview

We are looking for a Business Development Executive (BDE) who can build high-quality sales pipelines, qualify inbound and outbound opportunities, and convert discussions into project engagements. You will work closely with leadership, delivery, and marketing teams to keep growth consistent and measurable.

## What You Will Work On

- Identify and qualify leads across email, LinkedIn, referrals, and partner channels.
- Manage outreach campaigns and schedule discovery calls with potential clients.
- Understand client requirements and map them to TechMigos service offerings.
- Prepare proposals, pricing drafts, and follow-up communication.
- Maintain clean CRM updates and weekly progress reporting on pipeline health.

## Must-Have Qualifications

- 2+ years of business development or B2B sales experience in software services.
- Strong communication skills in written and spoken English.
- Proven experience in lead qualification and conversion workflows.
- Ability to coordinate with technical teams and explain solutions clearly.
- Familiarity with CRM tools, spreadsheets, and structured follow-up discipline.

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- Experience selling web, mobile, or SaaS product development services.
- Familiarity with proposal writing and basic effort estimation language.
- Existing network in startup or SME technology buyer communities.

## Hiring Process

- Intro call to understand your sales approach and deal handling experience.
- Practical round on lead qualification, outreach strategy, and objection handling.
- Final discussion focused on ownership, communication quality, and execution pace.

## Why Join TechMigos

- Direct impact on company growth and strategic client partnerships.
- High-ownership role with clear performance visibility.
- Remote-first work culture with fast decision making.
