## About the Role

As a Senior Full-Stack Engineer at TechMigos, you'll be instrumental in building software that makes a difference. You'll work closely with our design and strategy teams to deliver exceptional user experiences and robust backend systems.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the design and implementation of full-stack web applications
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct code reviews
- Collaborate with designers to translate Figma mockups into pixel-perfect code
- Architect scalable solutions and make key technical decisions
- Contribute to our internal tooling and development practices
- Communicate directly with clients on technical requirements and progress

## Requirements

- 5+ years of professional software development experience
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript and modern JavaScript frameworks (React, Vue, or Next.js)
- Experience with Node.js and RESTful API design
- Solid understanding of relational databases and SQL
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail
- Strong communication skills for client-facing work

## Nice to Have

- Experience with DevOps practices (CI/CD, containerization)
- Knowledge of machine learning or AI integration
- Previous agency or consulting experience
- Open source contributions

## What We Offer

- Competitive salary and equity compensation
- Remote-first culture with flexible hours
- $2,000 annual learning budget for courses and conferences
- Health, dental, and vision insurance
- Unlimited PTO policy
- Regular team retreats and events
