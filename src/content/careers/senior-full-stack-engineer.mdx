## Role Overview

We are looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer who can take ownership of product features across frontend, backend, and data layers. You will work closely with product, design, and engineering teammates to deliver stable, maintainable, and user-focused solutions.

## What You Will Work On

- Build production-grade features using modern JavaScript/TypeScript stacks.
- Design APIs and backend services that are reliable and easy to evolve.
- Create responsive, accessible interfaces with strong UX fundamentals.
- Improve performance, observability, and deployment confidence.
- Review code and mentor engineers through practical feedback.

## Must-Have Qualifications

- 5+ years of experience in full-stack product development.
- Strong command of React and Node.js in production use cases.
- Experience designing REST APIs, data models, and integration flows.
- Solid understanding of testing, debugging, and version control workflows.
- Ability to communicate technical tradeoffs clearly.

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- Experience with Astro, Next.js, or modern SSR frameworks.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and cloud deployment workflows.
- Experience improving Core Web Vitals and app performance at scale.

## Hiring Process

- Short introduction call focused on role fit.
- Technical interview with practical architecture and coding discussion.
- Final conversation on ownership, collaboration, and execution style.

## Why Join TechMigos

- High-ownership environment with meaningful engineering impact.
- Remote-first flexibility with collaborative delivery standards.
- Opportunities to shape architecture and mentor growing teams.
